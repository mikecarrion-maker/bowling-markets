🎳 Bowling Markets

How it works: Each player has a market — a bid and an offer, each with a size shown next to it (how much can be bet at that price right now). Hit the bid if you think the player will score below the bid. Lift the offer if you think they'll score above the offer. If your stake is bigger than the size shown, your bet goes to the market maker for approval before it's confirmed.
